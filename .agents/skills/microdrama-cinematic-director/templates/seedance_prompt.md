## Required Rules

- Write Seedance prompts in English.
- Keep Indonesian dialogue or narration in Bahasa Indonesia.
- Must include: Duration: 15 seconds.
- Must include: Aspect ratio: vertical 9:16.
- Must include: Mode: image-to-video.
- Must include: Audio: sound-on.
- Use the storyboard image as primary visual reference.
- Include dialogue/narration directly inside the prompt.
- Include ambience, music mood, and action-synced SFX when needed.
- Do not create separate voiceover instructions.
- Do not add random singing or unrelated music.
- Do not introduce new story events beyond the storyboard.
- If a block has 6 shots, adjust timing so total duration remains exactly 15 seconds.
