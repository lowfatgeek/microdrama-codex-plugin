## Shot Planning Rules

- 1 episode = 45 seconds.
- 1 episode = 3 blocks.
- 1 block = 15 seconds.
- Default: 5 shots × 3 seconds.
- Optional: 6 shots if B-roll is needed.
- Maximum B-roll: 2 shots per block.
- Total duration per block must remain exactly 15 seconds.
- Do not change the core story.
- Do not introduce new characters, locations, or reveals unless already in the approved script.

## Shot Types

Use these shot types:

```text
main
closeup
reaction
b-roll
insert
transition
reveal
cliffhanger
```
