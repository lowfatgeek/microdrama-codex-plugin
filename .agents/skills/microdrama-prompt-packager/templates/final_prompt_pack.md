## Packaging Rules

- Keep the prompt pack clean and copy-paste-ready.
- Do not add unnecessary explanations.
- Do not rewrite approved story/script/prompt content unless requested.
- Preserve English for image/video prompts.
- Preserve Indonesian dialogue/narration inside Seedance prompts.
- Make sure each episode has 3 storyboard prompts and 3 Seedance prompts.
