## Required Rules

- Do not write the full scripts yet unless the user asks.

- Episode 1 must create curiosity.
- Episode 2 must escalate mystery and conflict.
- Episode 3 must deliver reveal, revenge, and payoff.
- Use Bahasa Indonesia.
