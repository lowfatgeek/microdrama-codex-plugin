# EP_001_01 — Shot Plan

## Story Title
Gadis Miskin yang Ternyata Pemilik Hotel

## Episode Function
Episode 1 = Hook + Humiliation

---

# Block A — 0–15s

**Block Purpose:**  
Membangun hook visual dan menunjukkan Liana sebagai gadis sederhana yang masuk ke lingkungan mewah.

## Shot 1 — 0–3s
**Type:** main  
**Duration:** 3s  
**Visual:** Liana masuk ke restoran hotel mewah dengan dress putih sederhana.  
**Emotion:** tegang, canggung  
**Camera / Framing:** wide establishing shot, slow push-in  
**Character Focus:** Liana

## Shot 2 — 3–6s
**Type:** reaction  
**Duration:** 3s  
**Visual:** Tamu restoran kaya menoleh dan menilai penampilan Liana.  
**Emotion:** judgemental  
**Camera / Framing:** medium reaction shots  
**Character Focus:** rich guests

## Shot 3 — 6–9s
**Type:** closeup  
**Duration:** 3s  
**Visual:** Close-up wajah Liana yang tetap tenang meski diperhatikan.  
**Emotion:** calm under pressure  
**Camera / Framing:** tight close-up  
**Character Focus:** Liana

## Shot 4 — 9–12s
**Type:** main  
**Duration:** 3s  
**Visual:** Bu Ratna berdiri dari meja dan menatap Liana dari atas ke bawah.  
**Emotion:** contempt  
**Camera / Framing:** medium shot, low angle on Bu Ratna  
**Character Focus:** Bu Ratna

## Shot 5 — 12–15s
**Type:** cliffhanger  
**Duration:** 3s  
**Visual:** Bu Ratna mulai berkata kasar pada Liana di depan semua orang.  
**Emotion:** public humiliation  
**Camera / Framing:** over-the-shoulder shot from behind Liana  
**Character Focus:** Bu Ratna and Liana

---

# Block B — 15–30s

**Block Purpose:**  
Meningkatkan penghinaan publik dan memperlihatkan calon tunangan Liana tidak membelanya.

## Shot 1 — 15–18s
**Type:** main  
**Duration:** 3s  
**Visual:** Bu Ratna menunjuk pakaian Liana sambil berbicara keras.  
**Emotion:** harsh humiliation  
**Camera / Framing:** medium close-up  
**Character Focus:** Bu Ratna

## Shot 2 — 18–21s
**Type:** reaction  
**Duration:** 3s  
**Visual:** Calon tunangan Liana menunduk malu dan tidak berkata apa-apa.  
**Emotion:** disappointment  
**Camera / Framing:** close-up reaction  
**Character Focus:** fiancé

## Shot 3 — 21–24s
**Type:** reaction  
**Duration:** 3s  
**Visual:** Tamu restoran saling berbisik dan tertawa kecil.  
**Emotion:** social pressure  
**Camera / Framing:** quick reaction montage  
**Character Focus:** guests

## Shot 4 — 24–27s
**Type:** closeup  
**Duration:** 3s  
**Visual:** Liana menggenggam tangannya pelan, menahan sakit hati.  
**Emotion:** restrained sadness  
**Camera / Framing:** close-up on hands  
**Character Focus:** Liana

## Shot 5 — 27–30s
**Type:** main  
**Duration:** 3s  
**Visual:** Bu Ratna mengatakan Liana tidak pantas masuk keluarga mereka.  
**Emotion:** emotional attack  
**Camera / Framing:** two-shot, Bu Ratna dominant in frame  
**Character Focus:** Bu Ratna and Liana

---

# Block C — 30–45s

**Block Purpose:**  
Menutup episode dengan penghinaan paling besar dan cliffhanger identitas Liana.

## Shot 1 — 30–33s
**Type:** main  
**Duration:** 3s  
**Visual:** Bu Ratna menunjuk pintu keluar restoran.  
**Emotion:** rejection  
**Camera / Framing:** medium shot  
**Character Focus:** Bu Ratna

## Shot 2 — 33–36s
**Type:** closeup  
**Duration:** 3s  
**Visual:** Liana menatap Bu Ratna dengan tenang tanpa menangis.  
**Emotion:** quiet strength  
**Camera / Framing:** tight close-up  
**Character Focus:** Liana

## Shot 3 — 36–39s
**Type:** b-roll  
**Duration:** 3s  
**Visual:** Pintu restoran terbuka cepat, seorang manajer hotel masuk tergesa-gesa.  
**Emotion:** sudden interruption  
**Camera / Framing:** tracking shot toward doorway  
**Character Focus:** hotel manager

## Shot 4 — 39–42s
**Type:** reaction  
**Duration:** 3s  
**Visual:** Bu Ratna dan tamu lain terkejut melihat manajer hotel panik.  
**Emotion:** confusion  
**Camera / Framing:** reaction close-ups  
**Character Focus:** Bu Ratna and guests

## Shot 5 — 42–45s
**Type:** cliffhanger  
**Duration:** 3s  
**Visual:** Manajer hotel membungkuk sedikit pada Liana dan memanggilnya “Nona Liana”.  
**Emotion:** mystery reveal  
**Camera / Framing:** dramatic push-in on Liana  
**Character Focus:** Liana and hotel manager
