# Review Checklist

## Storyboard Review

- Character consistency
- Outfit consistency
- Correct panel count
- Shot accuracy
- Emotional clarity
- Cinematic realism
- No unwanted text
- No comic/manga/cartoon style

## Seedance Video Review

- Duration around 15 seconds
- Vertical 9:16
- Character consistency
- Natural motion
- Clear emotion
- Dialogue/narration audible
- Audio fits the scene
- No major deformity
- Ending connects to next block
